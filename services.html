FAQ'S

An FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) sheet is a document or website section that lists common questions and concise answers regarding a specific topic, product, or service. It aims to provide quick, self-service information, reducing support queries and increasing user trust. Common synonyms include FAQ page, knowledge base, or Q&A section.

1.Identify FAQs using data and customer insights. ... 2.Organize questions into logical categories. ... 3.Keep answers short, helpful, and clear. ... 4.Apply strong design principles. ... 5.Incorporate SEO. ... 6.Include digital assistants or self-service tools. ... 7.Maintain and update the FAQ page regularly.

Make your FAQ page easy to find by including it in your main navigation menu, your footer, and linking to it from your contact page

What is an FAQ page? A frequently asked questions page—or an FAQ page for short—is a key part of a knowledge base because it addresses your customers' most common questions and is useful at all stages of the customer journey. FAQs start with a question and then provide a concise answer.
